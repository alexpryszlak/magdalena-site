What we do

Three lines of support for federal buyers

One accountable point of contact for parts, supplies, and the engineering work that keeps them conforming.

Build-to-Print Parts

Machined and fabricated components produced to government technical data packages through vetted US machine shops, with founder-led engineering and quality oversight.

  • TDP and drawing review before quote
  • CNC machined, turned, and fabricated parts
  • First article and dimensional inspection
  • Material certs and traceability documentation

Industrial Supply

MRO and industrial products sourced from US manufacturers and distributors, with sourcing aligned to Buy American and Berry Amendment requirements.

  • MRO, hand and power tools
  • Fasteners: screws, bolts, studs, nuts, washers
  • Pipe, hose, and fittings
  • PPE and welding supplies

Engineering & Quality Services

Practical mechanical engineering and quality support from an engineer with shop-floor aerospace manufacturing experience.

  • Technical drawings and documentation (GD&T, CAD)
  • Reverse engineering support
  • Quality inspection and audit support
  • Calibration coordination
How an order moves

Engineering review up front, documentation at the end

Every build-to-print job is reviewed by an engineer before it is quoted and inspected before it ships.

01 / REVIEW

Read the TDP

Drawings, specs, revision levels, and inspection requirements are reviewed before pricing.

02 / QUOTE

Source & quote

Matched to a qualified US shop or supplier, with a clear quote returned quickly.

03 / BUILD

Oversee production

Engineering point of contact through manufacturing, with issues raised early.

04 / VERIFY

Inspect & deliver

Dimensional checks, certs, and paperwork verified against the contract before shipment.
